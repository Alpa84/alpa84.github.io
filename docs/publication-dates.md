## Reproducing the research

Use `git log --reverse --diff-filter=A --format="%h %aI %s" -- <path>` to locate creation commits, then inspect the content with `git show <commit>:<path>`. Compare homepage history with `git log --reverse -S'<link or identifying text>' -- index.html index_en.html en.html es.html`.

`git log --follow --name-status` is useful for genuine renames, such as the PASO map, but also follows copied templates. Used alone, it incorrectly attributes the prompt and API-signatures posts to the older originality article and the general-election map to the earlier PASO project.
